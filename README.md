I'm **Parzi Dinç**, a backend and systems developer based in Istanbul. I build APIs, automation tools and real-time services, and work on AI-powered products and iOS apps.

My work ranges from freelance integrations and scraping tools to community platforms and products of my own. I enjoy taking an idea through development, deployment and the everyday work of keeping it running.

### What I work on

- **Backend & APIs** — services, third-party integrations and data pipelines.
- **Automation & communities** — bots, streaming tools and workflows that save people time.
- **AI & mobile** — image generation products and native iOS experiences.
